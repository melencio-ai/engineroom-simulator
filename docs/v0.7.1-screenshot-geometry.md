# Engine Watch 3D v0.7.1 — screenshot-driven marine geometry cleanup

Status: **test feature branch**, branched from merged `main` v0.7.

## Scope and root-cause analysis

Windows PPSSPP captures of Deck 04 and Deck 02 exposed gaps in the presentation of the environment:

- **ECR approach:** an unguarded narrow bridge and black-looking space at the flanks of the upper forward machinery bulkhead;
- **Engine-side deck:** loosely overlaid rectangular rods and grating shapes that visually float over the green steel floor;
- **Open machinery well:** longitudinal rails existed but the transverse ends of the opening had no visible guardrails;
- **HUD:** an unsupported `|` character displayed as a question mark in the title.

This is an **environment geometry pass**, not a new machinery simulation. Deck elevations, stair centerlines, mission stations, interaction coordinates and process models are unchanged.

## Implementation

1. Split the Deck 01–04 machinery-side steel plates so the inner 0.82 m is now actual open grating, **not painted solid plate with a bar grid drawn on top**.
2. Place four contiguous 2.275 m modular OpenSteelGrating panels on each side of each working level; retain the v0.6 reusable 3D asset library. The open grating mesh has slightly denser, consistent longitudinal bars.
3. Add two continuous edge supports and five transverse bearing points per grating band so panels have visible structural seating.
4. Remove the legacy decorative rod overlays that created overlapping, disconnected-looking wire patterns.
5. Add two ECR bridge guardrails with toe protection, plus transverse railings across forward and aft ends of the open machinery well. They are deliberately positioned outside existing player navigation corridors.
6. Close the two unmodeled upper *flanking forward bulkhead bays* beside the central ECR (the ECR's doorway and viewing windows remain unobstructed). Lighten upper roof material from near-black to medium-coated steel.
7. Replace the unsupported ASCII pipe in the PSP HUD title with a dash, avoiding the rendered `?` glyph.

## Follow-up: floor surfaces disappearing while moving (Decks 01–04)

The additional Windows captures revealed a distinct surface-placement problem:

- Forty separate `CheckerPlate` objects had been stamped onto existing steel floor slabs. Their model's top was approximately **0.081 m above the deck**, leaving discontinuous raised rectangles across the walkway.
- Each open grating model was positioned with its upper surface **0.0456 m above nominal deck height**, rather than level with the adjacent plated walkway.
- Some steel bearer tops were proud of the floor, and eight grating panels were incorrectly drawn across the *solid* Level 00 tank top.
- A standalone steel plate also sat above the tank top as a duplicate deck surface.

Changes: replace the forty floating floor pieces with one **true inset** pump-flat service cover (0.60 × 1.00 m; its steel top equals the deck elevation), position all 32 legitimate Decks 01–04 grating bays with their tops exactly at the deck height, place bearer tops below the grate, remove tank-top grating overlays, and relocate the one reusable steel plate instance to the pipe-inspection bulkhead. The 33-part/16-finish catalog remains complete.

The PSP depth projection changes from 0.12–80 m to 0.25–60 m (desktop preview matches); this improves 16-bit depth buffer precision at shallow deck-viewing angles. It is a preventive rendering improvement, not proof that all visual disappearances originated from depth fighting.

A new C++ regression checks actual *horizontal triangle coverage* underneath multiple solid deck-gallery sample points on all levels, a physical top face under the inset service cover, the absence of tank-top grating, and exact grating/cover top elevations. Existing stair traversal, LO pump operation, and asset catalog tests remain.

## Manual acceptance checklist

1. From Deck 04 ECR, look toward the central doorway. The walkway should have clear handrails, no unguarded outer edge and a continuous floor into the machinery-side landing.
2. Turn to both sides of the ECR forward upper bulkhead. There should be opaque coated steel behind the beams, not a sky-colour void.
3. Walk past the main-engine side galleries on Deck 02. Grating must form continuous panels with visible frames, not independent rods suspended over a painted floor.
4. Check the engine well on Decks 01–04. Port/starboard longitudinal handrails and forward/aft transverse handrails should visually protect its opening. Emergency circulation should remain accessible.
5. Descend all decks and return without landing collision regression. Complete the main LO pump start/pressure objective and check the strainer interaction.
6. Confirm the top HUD renders **ENGINE WATCH 3D -** instead of a `?` glyph.
